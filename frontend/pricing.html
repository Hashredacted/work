Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Marg ERP license a one-time payment or recurring?+
The software license is a one-time fee. After the first year of free support, you can optionally renew an AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) for continued updates and support.
What is the difference between Marg ERP and MargCloud?+
Marg ERP 9+ is a desktop software installed on your local computer. MargCloud is the same ERP hosted on the cloud — you access it from any browser or device without installing anything.
Do the mobile apps require a separate purchase?+
Most mobile apps (eRetail, eOrder, eOwner) are integrated add-ons. The eOwner app is free with the Gold edition. Other apps have their own annual subscription pricing as listed above.
Can I switch from desktop ERP to MargCloud later?+
Yes! Your existing data can be migrated to MargCloud. Contact us and our team will assist with the migration at no additional data migration cost.
Do you offer a free trial or demo?+
Absolutely! We offer a free demo session where our team will walk you through the software with your actual business data. Click "Book a Free Demo" to schedule.
